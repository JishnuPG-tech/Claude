# Claude Settings Specification

## 1. Structure & Sections
The Settings screen organizes preferences into intuitive categories adhering to Material Design 3 and Claude aesthetics:

### 1.1 Account & Plan
- Profile Avatar with Initials.
- Name & Email display.
- Current Plan pill: "Claude Pro" (with terracotta badge).
- Usage bar: "Usage limit reset at 4:00 PM".

### 1.2 Appearance
- Theme: Radio option for `System default`, `Light`, or `Dark`.

### 1.3 Model & Cognitive Preferences
- Default Model: `Claude 3.7 Sonnet`, `Claude 3.5 Sonnet`, `Claude 3.5 Haiku`.
- Extended Thinking: Switch toggle to enable/disable reasoning.
- Thinking Budget: Slider for maximum token allocation (e.g. 4,000 - 32,000 tokens).

### 1.4 Voice Preferences
- Voice selection list: Buttery, Airy, Mellow, Glassy, Rounded (with play sample button).
- Auto-read spoken responses toggle.

### 1.5 Connectors & MCP (Model Context Protocol)
- Toggle list of enabled external tools (Terminal, Web Search, GitHub, Postgres).
- Connection status indicator (Connected / Disconnected).

### 1.6 About & Legal
- App version: "Claude for Android v1.2.0".
- Anthropic Privacy Policy & Terms of Service links.
